vindOS installs no NVIDIA software. To use Fullscreen and VR games you download the NVIDIA CloudXR Runtime and Stream Manager yourself from NVIDIA NGC and accept NVIDIA's terms; vindOS only reads the archives you place in its CloudXR folder.

For OpenVR games you choose, vindOS replaces the game's openvr_api.dll with the OpenComposite bridge and keeps the original beside it; Settings restores the originals at any time, and uninstalling restores them.
